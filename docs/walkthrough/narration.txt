FiLMeR turns a current GFS forecast into a high-resolution regional field, entirely in your browser.

Choose one of four trained domains, select temperature, wind, humidity, pressure, or precipitation, and request up to eight three-hour timestamps.

The app downloads the live GFS tensors, verifies them, builds the model input, and runs ONNX inference locally.

The result is a pan-and-zoom map with one fixed color scale across every frame, so colors remain comparable as the animation advances.

The web version uses ONNX Runtime Web, while the iPhone and Android apps use native ONNX Runtime. Forecast fields stay on your device.

FiLMeR is a research emulator, not an operational warning service.
