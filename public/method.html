Method & validation

What FiLMeR does—and what it does not.

FiLMeR v1.0 is a conditional regional emulator. It converts a pair of GFS atmospheric states plus static geography into six 2-D regional surface fields. It is not a complete GFS or WRF replacement.

One forecast step

GFS at t−3 h and t→ Exact crop + normalization→ FiLMeR at t+3 h

Longer products repeat this independent conditional step using the corresponding GFS sequence. FiLMeR outputs are never fed back into the model.

Validated scope

Geography

Only the four trained domains: India at 27 km and three trained regional domains at 9 km.

Time

3-hour cadence, with 1, 2, 4 or 8 timestamps in this public interface.

Output

Fixed 99 × 99 grids for T2, U10, V10, Q2, surface pressure and precipitation.

Answers to operational questions

QuestionEvidence-backed answer
Arbitrary location?No. Architecture-level conditioning does not validate unseen geography.
Arbitrary resolution?No. The shared model was trained at 27 km and 9 km; 1 km requires suitable training data and held-out validation.
Hourly or 96-hour standalone?No. Current cadence is 3 hours and every output remains conditioned on GFS.
Full 3-D atmospheric state?No. FiLMeR v1.0 emits six 2-D surface fields, not pressure-level or native-model-level fields.
Operational?Research prototype. Do not use it as safety-critical weather guidance.

Timing boundary

The browser’s wait is primarily data movement. A measured one-timestamp run took 43.5 s end to end: about 41.2 s for current GFS data and 0.92 s for model inference on the tested machine. This is not directly comparable to a WRF end-to-end benchmark unless acquisition, preprocessing, variables, levels and outputs are aligned.
